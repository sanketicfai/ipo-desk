How this works: every 15 minutes (:00 / :15 / :30 / :45 IST) the GitHub Actions workflow scrape.yml runs daily_ipo_update.py → closing_day_tracker.py, which reads the live BSE & NSE bid data (as published by IPOJi) and appends one snapshot per IPO — share-wise (offer vs applied), application-wise (reserve vs applied) and ₹-demand — to qib_closing_log.json (mirrored to qib_intraday_log.json, which the workflow commits), until bidding closes at 5:00 PM IST. This page re-fetches the log every 60 s. Manual trigger from a branch: gh workflow run scrape.yml --ref <branch>. Figures are market bid data, not investment advice.
